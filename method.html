Betwinner analiz yöntemi

2026-08-04 10:39 UTC · bu sayfa koddan üretilir: her eşleştirme ve her sayı engine/, config.py ve research/ içinden okunur, elle yazılmaz.
Dürüst özet. Katalogdaki 64 spor dalının tamamı araştırıldı ve 1261 istatistik→bahis eşleştirmesi kayıtlı. Bunlardan bugün 1 tanesi modelli (Masa Tenisi) — yani yalnızca bu sporlardan seçim çıkar. Kalanlarda güvenlik merdiveni basamak kurabiliyor ama yönü söyleyecek model yok, ve yön fiyattan okunamayacağı için seçim de çıkmaz. Uzun bir tablonun aksini ima etmesine izin vermemek için bu en başta yazıyor: aradaki fark, yol haritasının kendisidir.

Analiz nasıl çalışıyor

  1. Kart çekilir. Betwinner'ın kendi LineFeed'inden önümüzdeki 48 saatin tüm maç öncesi marketleri: spor → turnuva → maç → market. Son çekimde 674.379 satır / 3.627 maç.
  2. Yapısal eleme. İkinci katılımcısı olmayan kayıt bir "karşı karşıya" maçı değildir — turnuva şampiyonu, seçim sorusu, çok koşuculu yarış hepsi tek kuralla düşer. Aynı gün sonuçlanmayan sporlar (MULTI_DAY_SPORTS) ve sonucu tahmin edilemeyen kumar marketleri (EXCLUDED_SPORTS) de çıkar.
  3. Model yönü söyler. Fiyat asla. Kısa bir oran, olasılık tahmini artı kitabın marjı artı kitabın pozisyonudur; onu olasılık diye okumak kitabın görüşünü kitaba geri vermektir.
  4. Merdiven biçimi seçer. Aynı görüşün en güvenli ifadesi: "kazanır" yerine "kaybetmez", sonuç yerine en büyük artı handikap, manşet total yerine en düşük üst çizgisi.
  5. Oran tam olarak bir kez okunur: MIN_ODDS = 1.10 eşiği. Merdiven bu eşiği geçen en güvenli basamakta durur.
  6. Güven tabanı. Model MIN_MODEL_SURVIVAL = %75 altında kalan her şeyi eler. Taban olmasa merdiven, yazı-turanın en güvenli biçimini döndürürdü — ki o hâlâ yazı-turadır.
  7. İadeli marketler kapalı. Tam sayı handikaplar, çeyrek handikaplar ve tam sayı totaller stakeyi geri verebilir; sadece .5 çizgileri kalır. Böylece güven tabanı "hayatta kalma" değil kazanma tabanı olur.
  8. Maç başına tek seçim, sonra 1'den N'e puana göre numaralandırma.
Modelin güvenli bir görüşü olmayan maç hiçbir şey üretmez. Listeyi doldurmak için "en az kötü" seçeneği eklemek, işin tamamını anlamsızlaştırırdı. Son kartta 1356 maç tarandı, 37 seçim çıktı; geri kalanın büyük kısmı modelsiz (995 maç).

Puanlama

puan = 100 × ( 0.75 + (modelin olasılığı − 0.75) × kanıt )

Puan, modelin bu bahsi kazanma olasılığıdır. 92 puan "model bunun yaklaşık %92 tutacağını söylüyor" demektir. Kitabın fiyatı puana hiç girmez — bir test bunu doğrular: oranı değiştirip modeli sabit tuttuğunuzda puan kıpırdamaz.

Kanıt tek düzeltmedir ve tamamen bize aittir: takım/oyuncu isim eşleşmesinin gücü, ligin fit edildiği maç sayısı, derecelendirme kovasının arkasındaki örneklem. Aynı olasılıktaki iki seçim eşit sağlamlıkta değildir; kanıt zayıfsa olasılık eşiğe doğru geri çekilir ve ne kadar çekildiği "−x puan" olarak satırda yazar.

Puanlar %75 ile %100 arasında dar bir bantta toplanır, çünkü listeye giren her seçim zaten eşiği geçmiştir. Bu bant ürünün kendisidir; kozmetik olarak germek modelin gerçekte ayıramadığı %91 ile %93 arasında yapay bir fark uydurmak olurdu.

VERIFIED AGAINST THE LIVE FEED: Betwinner's pre-match LineFeed exposes exactly ONE market per race — the win market (horse T=2637/G=26, trot T=6414/G=7571), with GE and AE empty on every race sampled. No place, each-way, forecast, tricast, match-bet or ante-post market appeared in the feed, and sports 132/151/133 returned zero meetings. The mappings below are therefore written for completeness and for the site UI, but ONLY win is confirmed present in the data this project actually ingests.
each way note
An each-way bet is TWO bets and the place half is a different question from the win half. Field size is a FIRST-CLASS input here, not context: it sets the number of paid places and the odds fraction. The runner type that wins the place half is the consistent, trouble-free, reliable-finishing type — high gait-break rate or a habitual trouble-getter is far more damaging to the place half than to the win half, because the place half is sold on reliability.
forecast note
Forecast/tricast needs a JOINT distribution over finishing order, not N independent win probabilities. Naive Harville/Plackett-Luce from win probabilities systematically misprices these because it ignores the correlation that pace and draw create: two front-runners drawn together do not finish 1-2, they cut each other's throats. This is the market where trap/draw/pace matter most and where a win-probability model transfers worst. GBGB publishes historical raceForecast and raceTricast dividends, which is a rare free calibration target.
match bet note
THE ONLY TWO-OUTCOME MARKET IN RACING, and therefore the ONLY place the project's safety ladder can apply — everywhere else the outcome space is the whole field and there is no 'safer form of the same view' to walk to. Two further cautions specific to match bets: (a) they are usually settled on which runner finishes AHEAD, so a non-completion (fell, pulled up, disqualified, gait break) resolves the bet rather than voiding it — the gait-break rate is a direct input, not a footnote; (b) a match bet normally VOIDS if either runner is a non-runner, which makes it the one racing market where non-runner risk is refunded rather than deducted. Both must be confirmed against Betwinner's terms.
ante post note
The dominant variable in an ante-post price is NOT ability — it is PARTICIPATION PROBABILITY. Because the stake is normally lost if the runner never starts, the correct model is P(runs) x P(wins | runs), and P(runs) depends on entry stage, alternative engagements, ground preference versus the forecast going, and trainer intent. Modelling ante-post with the same function as a same-day win bet is the single biggest category error available here. Note also: all three AntePost sport ids (132/151/133) returned ZERO meetings in the live pull, so there is currently nothing to model.

araştırma notları
CHESS IS THREE-WAY AND THE DRAW WINS. Measured on 35,538 real OTB games from June 2026: draw rate rises monotonically with strength - 18.8% below 2000, 34.1% at 2200-2399, 41.8% at 2400-2499, 51.7% at 2600-2699. Above 2600 the draw is the single most likely result. Every downstream market (handicap, correct score, totals) is a function of this one number.
DRAW RATE IS A FUNCTION OF THE CLOCK, MEASURED ON A MATCHED POPULATION: 41.6% classical / 35.4% rapid / 23.1% blitz for the same 2200+ players in the same month. Colour advantage moves the same way (0.546 -> 0.561 -> 0.512). A blitz market and a classical market between the same two players share nothing but the names.
THE LICHESS BROADCAST DATABASE IS THE BEST SOURCE FOUND FOR THIS SPORT - 1,146,297 elite OTB games with FIDE IDs, Elo, ECO, and per-move Stockfish [%eval] on 84% of games. It makes average centipawn loss computable without running an engine, which is the one quality metric that leads the rating instead of lagging it. Licensed CC-BY-SA 4.0 (attribution + share-alike), NOT the CC0 that covers the rest of database.lichess.org - the licences differ per file and must not be conflated.
THE BIGGEST FREE CHESS DATASET IS THE WRONG DATASET. Lichess's 7,949,495,674 online games are CC0 and free of any restriction, but online draw rates measured at 2.7-7.0% run 5-10x below OTB rates for comparable time controls, largely because 33.2% of online games end on time forfeit. It is superb for engine/opening work and actively dangerous as a calibration set for an OTB match price.
TIME CONTROL IS THE JOIN KEY AND IT IS MISSING FROM THE MAIN GAME FEED. TWIC PGN carries no TimeControl tag at all and the broadcast tag was populated on only ~28% of games, in inconsistent units ('90+30' minutes vs '5400+30' seconds vs '90 mins + 30 Secs'). chess-results.com states it explicitly per tournament and is the fix. Classify with the FIDE base + 60 x increment formula, never the event name.
POKER IS NOT MODELLABLE PRE-MATCH AND THE REASON IS STRUCTURAL, NOT TECHNICAL. The largest free live database holds 6,563,609 results across 740,492 events - but a 'result' is a CASH. Entries that busted are never recorded, so the denominator of ITM rate, ROI and every skill metric does not exist in the data. Combine that with an unknown final field size at bet time and final-table deals that decouple the official winner from competitive play, and there is no free-data path to a poker outright price. Recommend not building it.
THE ONE POKER SOURCE THAT WORKS IS WSOP.COM - fully open robots.txt, no Cloudflare, live schedule, past-results archive and a POY leaderboard, all verified by body. Everything else in poker is blocked: pokerdb.thehendonmob.com and globalpokerindex.com are Cloudflare 403 from our egress, and pokerstarslive.com simply has no results archive (genuine 404s, not a block).
STATUS CODES LIED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS IN THIS RESEARCH. chessgames.com returned 403 to a library User-Agent and 200 with real content to a browser one - a UA check, not a block. Conversely pokerdb, globalpokerindex and 365chess all serve Cloudflare challenge bodies, and pokerdb served one even on /robots.txt. FIDE returns HTTP 404 for robots.txt with a 9.5KB HTML body. Every source in this file was qualified on its bytes.
TWO SOURCES ARE ROBOTS-DISALLOWED AND WERE NOT FETCHED: api.chess.com blocks its entire host ('User-agent: * / Disallow: /'), and lichess.org disallows /api/ and /game/export/. Neither is a technical failure and both would otherwise be valuable. chessgames.com names GPTBot and ChatGPT-User but not anthropic-ai, and disallows /perl/chess.pl - which is precisely its head-to-head interface, so H2H must be built by intersecting pgnmentor career archives instead.
FIDE PUBLISHES THREE SEPARATE RATING LISTS AND TWO FREE UNCERTAINTY SIGNALS THAT ARE ROUTINELY IGNORED. Standard, rapid and blitz are distinct downloads and distinct pools. The <k> field (40/20/10) tells you how converged a rating is and the <flag> field tells you it is stale - 167,396 of 306,251 blitz-rated players are flagged inactive. Both are free and both belong in the prior's variance.

This shifts the whole three-way distribution in the final round, it is knowable in advance from chess-results standings, and it is invisible to any model built on ratings alone.

safety ladder note
These are nominally two-outcome markets, so the project's safety ladder would normally treat the moneyline as the safe base. That reading is only valid where a draw is genuinely impossible. Measured draw/no-contest mass: SUMO effectively 0% (no hikiwake since 1974; inconclusive bouts are re-fought, not split) - moneyline IS a safe two-way base. MMA 1.76% (65 draws + 90 no contests in 8784 UFC bouts) - small but real, and no-contests can appear MONTHS later via overturned results. BOXING materially higher and, decisively, Betwinner prices it explicitly as 1X2 with an X. Never fold a boxing 1X2 into two-way implied probabilities.
araştırma notları
The MMA data situation is far better than the boxing one, and this asymmetry should drive product scope. One free, fresh, unauthenticated mirror (Greco1899/scrape_ufc_stats) delivers 41340 PER-ROUND rows over 8647 bouts with knockdowns, significant strikes landed/attempted split by target and position, takedowns, submission attempts and control time - every MMA statistic requested. Boxing has no free equivalent at all: BoxRec is contractually closed and CompuBox is commercial.
VERIFICATION LESSON, EXACTLY AS THE PROJECT'S RULE PREDICTS: ufcstats.com returns HTTP 200 with content-type text/html and a 2998-byte body that is a SHA-256 proof-of-work JS interstitial ('Checking your browser...'), containing zero data. Any qualifier checking status codes would have recorded the primary MMA source as working. The open GitHub mirror of that same site is fully reachable - so the fix was a different host, not a workaround.
sumo-api.com is the single best source in this research: free, unauthenticated, no key, fresh to 2026-07-14, and one call to /api/basho/{id}/banzuke/{division} returns banzuke rank, per-day results, kimarite, fusen defaults AND kyujo absences together. Sumo is the best-instrumented and cleanest of the seven ids despite being the most obscure.
Sumo is also the only one of these sports where the two-way moneyline is a genuinely safe base: no hikiwake since 1974, and inconclusive bouts are RE-FOUGHT (torinaoshi) rather than split. Boxing sits at the opposite extreme - Betwinner prices an explicit 1X2 with an X - and MMA is in between at a measured 1.76% draw-plus-no-contest rate.
THREE COMPLIANCE BLOCKS BY NAME, NONE OF WHICH WERE FETCHED: Tapology disallows ClaudeBot / anthropic-ai / Claude-Web / Claudebot explicitly (four separate entries plus an EU DSM Article 4 reservation), and ESPN disallows anthropic-ai. Losing Tapology costs the best free scheduled-bout and short-notice-replacement metadata, which is why that statistic now depends on Wikipedia prose extraction.
BoxRec is closed on BOTH axes and the finding is unambiguous: its robots.txt is itself unreadable behind a Cloudflare 403 challenge, and its Terms prohibit 'the use of automated systems or software to extract data' absent a written licence agreement. The contractual bar survives any technical workaround. Wikipedia's Infobox boxer is the legal substitute and carries total/wins/KO/losses/draws/no_contests consistently across every boxer tested.
The finish hazard is measurably front-loaded and this is the most exploitable structural fact in the file: R1 27.4%, R2 16.5%, R3 8.0% of all 3-round bouts. A uniform-hazard model will systematically misprice both ends of the totals ladder.
Counter-intuitively, longer fights reach the cards LESS often: 38.9% of 5-round championship bouts go the distance versus 48.2% of 3-round bouts. Extra rounds add finish opportunities faster than they add survivors.
A complete free judging-tendency dataset is hiding in a free-text column: ufc_fight_results.csv's DETAILS field carries named judges and their individual scorecards for 4035 of 4091 decisions (98.6%), alongside 243 distinct named referees. Judging and stoppage tendencies - normally treated as unmeasurable - are directly estimable for the busiest officials.
Home-crowd bias is real, quantified and METHOD-DEPENDENT: Balmer, Nevill & Lane (2005) found equally matched boxers had a 0.74 home win probability on points versus 0.57 by knockout. It belongs in the decision branch of a method model, not as a flat moneyline adjustment.
Half-round settlement marks differ by discipline (2:30 MMA, 1:30 boxing, 1:00 bare-knuckle) AND the halfway-versus-end-of-round convention differs by book. Betwinner's own rules pages 404 from here, so this is an unresolved settlement risk sitting under every totals market across four of the seven sport ids.

The three richest scrapeable cricket stats sites are all closed - which is precisely why Cricsheet carries the project.
WEATHER IS A FIRST-CLASS MODELLING INPUT IN CRICKET, NOT A GARNISH, AND IT IS FREE. Open-Meteo returns hourly dew point, humidity and precipitation probability with no API key (verified in body). It is the only way to make three separate exposures quantitative: dew advantage to the chasing side in day-night matches, DLS-reversal risk, and no-result void risk - plus the Test draw price, which is substantially a weather forecast. Chain it via Cricsheet venue -> Wikidata coordinates (SPARQL verified) -> Open-Meteo.
THE TWO HEADLINE TRAPS COMPOUND EACH OTHER. Rain does not just add variance to a totals line, it splits the outcome space three ways: play through (settle normally), reduced/DLS (settle on resources, where the side behind on runs can win), or abandon below 20 overs an ODI / 5 overs a T20 (void, no settlement at all). These are three different payoff regimes, and the threshold itself moves - World Cup knockouts require 10 overs a side, not 5.
MATCH TOTAL RUNS IS RIGHT-CENSORED BECAUSE THE CHASE STOPS AT THE TARGET, AND TEAM TOTAL EXCEEDS THE SUM OF BATTER RUNS BECAUSE OF EXTRAS. Two structural biases, both trivially avoidable, both fatal if missed: the first overprices the over on match totals, the second breaks any attempt to reconcile team-total and batsman markets. Model the first innings uncensored and the second innings truncated.
METHOD OF DISMISSAL IS THE CLEANEST STAT-TO-MARKET MAPPING IN THE SPORT - Cricsheet's wickets[].kind gives caught, bowled, lbw, run out, stumped and the rest directly (all six observed in a single sampled Test). The same field is what stops top-bowler markets being wrong: run outs are credited to no bowler and must be filtered out.
THE BIG DATA GAP IS PLAYER ATTRIBUTES, NOT MATCH EVENTS. Bowling type and batter handedness appear NOWHERE in the Cricsheet JSON (confirmed by inspecting info and every delivery). Spin-vs-pace matchups - among the largest real edges in cricket - therefore require an external player-attribute table joined on the Cricsheet register id, and the obvious source for it is the one that is robots-blocked.
FOUR FORMATS, FOUR DIFFERENT GAMES - AND ONE OF THEM HAS NO OVERS. Test cricket is a three-outcome market with declarations and follow-ons; The Hundred has 100 balls, 10-ball end changes and a 20-BALL bowler cap, so every over-based metric is undefined for it. Cricsheet's info.balls_per_over, match_type and event fields make the filter exact - use them before aggregating anything, or 322 Hundred matches will silently poison the T20 priors.
TWO 2025 RULE CHANGES INVALIDATE OLDER TRAINING DATA AT THE DEATH: the ODI ball change (two new balls only to over 34, then one chosen ball for 35-50, ICC 27 June 2025) and the stop clock with its five-run penalty, now extended to Tests. Cut the ODI death-overs training window at the ball change or the model is fitted on a different sport.

ladder note
GOLF IS A FIELD SPORT, NOT A TWO-SIDED ONE. A 156-player outright is a 156-outcome market, so the engine/ladder.py logic — walk a fixed direction from aggressive to safe — has almost nothing to walk down. TWO-BALLS AND THREE-BALLS ARE THE ONLY NEAR-TWO-OUTCOME MARKETS IN GOLF AND THEREFORE THE ONLY PLACE A SAFETY LADDER CAN APPLY: a two-ball is a genuine head-to-head (two outcomes, or three where a tie is priced), and a three-ball is a 3-way market that behaves like football's 1X2 with no draw-equivalent to hide in. Everything else — outright, top 5/10/20, FRL, nationality, group — is a many-outcome market where 'safer' means only 'more places', which is not the same guarantee and is precisely where dead-heat reduction eats the payout. SEPARATELY AND MORE IMPORTANTLY: 'TO MAKE THE CUT' IS THE SAFEST GENUINE MARKET IN GOLF AND THE NATURAL LADDERING TARGET. It is binary (made/missed), it resolves for ~half the field, it is driven by the stablest stats in the sport (SG:T2G and bogey avoidance rather than putting variance), and it does not suffer dead-heat reduction. Treat it as the base rung; treat two-balls and three-balls as the only true head-to-head rungs; do not construct a ladder out of top-5 -> top-10 -> top-20, because those rungs are not strictly safer once dead heat is priced in.

DEAD HEAT is the largest silent modelling error in golf betting. Four players tied for 10th turns a +550 top-10 into an 87.50 profit on a 100 stake, and 20-30% of top-10 bets hit some dead heat. A golf scanner that ranks selections on quoted price without a dead-heat haircut will systematically favour exactly the markets where the haircut is worst.

STRUCTURALLY, GOLF IS THE OPPOSITE OF THIS REPO'S OTHER SPORTS. Every existing ladder assumes two or three outcomes; a 156-player outright has 156. 14 sources verified by reading the actual response body; 17 recorded as blocked or abstained, 6 of those for compliance rather than technical failure. Two hosts disallow Claude agents BY NAME: fpf.pt (Portuguese FA, which runs the Liga Placard futsal championship) and org.football.ch.
The verification rule earned its keep twice. lnfs.es returns HTTP 200 with a 4 KB 'we are renovating' placeholder, and UEFA's registry lists ~50 domestic futsal leagues whose match feed returns a literal empty array. Both would have passed a status-code check and both are worthless.
Three unrelated feeds independently confirm the same settlement convention: the shootout is stored OUTSIDE the goals total (BSWW pso, UEFA score.penalty, FIFA HomeTeamPenaltyScore) while extra-time goals are stored INSIDE it. That convergence is strong evidence the convention is the industry norm and not a quirk of one provider.
match.uefa.com/v5/matches is the best single source across all three sports - it is the only one that hands over an explicit settlement enum (WIN_REGULAR / DRAW / WIN_ON_EXTRA_TIME / WIN_ON_PENALTIES) alongside separated regular, total, penalty and aggregate scores. It covers futsal only.
beachsoccer.com's WordPress API is the sleeper find: an open, unauthenticated, paginated feed with the three period scores, extra time and shootout as separate fields, plus per-player fouls and cards. It made real beach soccer baselines computable in one session.
Own goals are 3.7% of all UEFA futsal goals, roughly double the football rate, driven by deflections off a five-a-side block and by flying-goalkeeper turnovers into an empty net. That is about a quarter of a goal per match that a football-derived totals model does not know exists.
Beach soccer's period split is 29.5 / 32.6 / 36.0 and futsal's half split is 45.8 / 53.8 - neither sport divides evenly, and in futsal the skew is score-state driven so it widens with the spread rather than staying constant.
The most valuable handball statistics on the open web are on IHF match-centre pages, server-rendered and robots-permitted, carrying Wing/6m/9m/7M shot splits, Fastbreak, Shot Efficiency and 2-Min counts. They cover international tournaments only - the club game is the gap.
Compliance cut deeper here than in any previous sport in this project: fpf.pt names ClaudeBot in robots.txt, rfebm.com disallows exactly /competiciones/, eurohandball.com disallows the /umbraco/ path where all its match data lives, and beachsoccer.com disallows the PDF match sheets. In each case the blocked path was the interesting one.
Two dead URL families to purge from any inherited config: the Handball-Bundesliga has moved liquimoly-hbl.de -> daikin-hbl.de -> opel-hbl.de (and hbl.de now serves a self-signed cert), and the French league is now the Daikin StarLigue with the liqui-moly-starligue paths returning 404.
TheSportsDB fails these sports the same way the volleyball research found it failing there: it has five handball leagues, zero futsal, zero beach soccer, and no half-time fields - so it cannot serve any half or period market.
TWO WIDELY REPEATED 'FACTS' WERE CHECKED AGAINST PRIMARY DOCUMENTS AND ARE FALSE. (1) The EHF Champions League does NOT use an away-goals rule and does NOT play extra time in two-legged ties - the string 'away goal' appears nowhere in its 107-page regulations, and art. 10.3 says '(no extra time will be played)'. Search summaries assert the opposite, apparently by conflating UEFA football with EHF handball. (2) Beach soccer's 'the fouled player must take the free kick' rule was REMOVED in the 2025-26 Laws and replaced by 'any player who was on the pitch when the offence was committed' - and FIFA's own public rules guide still publishes the superseded version. Neither error is detectable without reading the rulebooks.
BEACH SOCCER'S SECONDS RULE IS FOUR, NOT FIVE. Verified in both the 2025-26 and 2024-25 FIFA Laws, and there is additionally a separate TEAM-level four-second rule for keeping the ball in one's own penalty area. Any five-second constant is wrong.
THE BUZZER IS NOT THE SETTLEMENT BOUNDARY IN ANY OF THE THREE. Handball Rule 2:4, Futsal Law 7.2 and Beach Soccer Law 7.2 all extend the period for a pending free throw / DFKSAF / free kick or penalty, and in beach soccer a shot that hits a post, bar or keeper during that extension IS a goal. Conversely handball Rule 2:9 requires a goal scored after the timekeeper's whistle to be DISALLOWED. Goals can be added, and deleted, after time expires.
AN EHF FORFEIT SETTLES AT A FABRICATED 0:10, retroactively if an ineligible player is discovered after the fact - it is not a void. That is a settlement outcome no pricing model produces and no bettor bet into, and it is the sharpest administrative risk found across the three sports.

ordering note
head_to_head_* first by design - it is the only two-outcome market across all five sports and therefore the only place a safety-ladder / staking progression can be applied at all. Everything else is multi-outcome and cannot support one.
araştırma notları
F1 is exceptionally well served for free and is the only one of the five sports where a serious model can be built today: jolpica (results/quali/sprint/pitstops, current to 2026 round 10), openf1 (sector times, tyre compound, weather, safety-car messages), livetiming.formula1.com (the FastF1 backend, reachable) and the FIA documents portal all verified by body.
The FIA documents portal is the most important source in this file and the one a naive build would omit entirely - it is the only place publishing PROVISIONAL and FINAL classification as separate PDFs, which is precisely the distinction every head-to-head, podium and points bet settles on.
Two 403s in this research were NOT blocks: a guessed livetiming session path returns S3 AccessDenied, and a guessed NASCAR race_id returns S3 AccessDenied. Both look exactly like a hostile block and both are really 404s. Conversely cyclingstage returns HTTP 200 with a '404 - page not found' title for unknown slugs. Status codes were wrong in both directions here - only the body settled it.
SPEEDWAY IS EFFECTIVELY UNSOURCEABLE. Every federation and official site checked - fimspeedway.com, speedwayekstraliga.pl, fim-moto.com - disallows ClaudeBot or anthropic-ai by name in robots.txt, and speedwaygp.com is a dead domain. This is a compliance wall, not a technical one, and no amount of retrying from Actions changes it.
CYCLING IS THE SECOND-WORST GAP. ProCyclingStats is blocked twice over (Cloudflare challenge on robots.txt itself, plus a public statement that they do not offer API access and decline data requests) and FirstCycling is Cloudflare-blocked. The permitted remainder - cyclingstage, Wikipedia, LeTourDataSet, open-meteo - covers stage profiles, results and crosswinds but not per-rider historical depth.
Head-to-head is the only two-outcome market in any of these five sports, so it is the only place a safety ladder can apply - and it is also the market with the nastiest settlement asymmetry, where DNS voids and DNF loses. The market best suited to a staking progression is the one most likely to be graded in a way a naive model did not predict.
DNF risk is a TEAM property in F1 (mechanical, correlated across teammates) but a RIDER property in MotoGP (crashes, uncorrelated). The same feature name means opposite things in sport 26 and sport 31 and the models must not share it.

ladder note
ALL FIVE OF THESE SPORTS ARE FIELD SPORTS BY DEFAULT, NOT TWO-SIDED ONES. A swimming final is 8 outcomes, a weightlifting category is 10-15, a shooting final is 8, an Olympic sailing fleet is 20-40 boats over a multi-day series, and a surfing event is a 24-36 surfer draw. HEAD-TO-HEAD MATCHUPS ARE THE ONLY TWO-OUTCOME MARKET IN THIS FILE AND THEREFORE THE ONLY PLACE A SAFETY LADDER CAN APPLY - the engine/ladder.py idea of walking a fixed direction from aggressive to safe needs a market with a monotone risk axis, and only a head-to-head has one. Everything else (outright, podium/top-3, medal counts, nation markets) is a many-outcome market where 'safer' means only 'more places', which is a different guarantee and is exactly where dead-heat reduction eats the payout. TWO FURTHER WARNINGS SPECIFIC TO THIS FILE: (1) a head-to-head here is only genuinely two-outcome if the book has defined what happens when NEITHER athlete records a result - two weightlifters who both bomb out, two swimmers who are both disqualified, two surfers whose heat is abandoned - and that is a void condition, not a third price, so confirm it before laddering. (2) SURFING IS THE ONE SPORT HERE WITH A NATIVE HEAD-TO-HEAD STRUCTURE: from round two onward every Olympic and WSL heat is two surfers, winner advances, loser out. That makes surfing the most ladder-friendly sport of the five in market STRUCTURE and simultaneously the least modellable in DATA, which is an uncomfortable combination and should be treated as a reason for caution rather than an invitation.

AND SHOOTING HAS A SECOND, LESS OBVIOUS TRAP: qualification RANK does not determine final ENTRY. SURFING'S 2026 FORMAT CHANGE INVALIDATES ANY PRIOR BUILT ON 2021-2025. The WSL Finals playoff is abolished; the title is decided on season points, best 9 of 12, with Pipeline worth 15,000 points (1.5x a normal event). There is also no mid-season cut and no elimination rounds - lose your first heat and you are out of the event. The outright title market has changed from a one-day playoff into a dropped-results points race.
CONDITION-DEPENDENCE IS MEASURABLE IN CALENDAR DAYS, NOT AS A VAGUE RISK. Olympic surfing at Teahupo'o 2024 was scheduled for 27-30 July with a 5-day contingency; it finished on 5 August after cancellations on 29 July, 3 August and 4 August - the entire contingency was consumed. penalty corners earned
Short corners awarded to a team, available PER QUARTER.
bu bahis için: THE field hockey statistic. Penalty corners are a set-piece scoring route with a conversion rate several times open-play efficiency, so corner volume forecasts goals far better than shot volume does.
penalty corners converted
Goals scored from penalty corners; derivable as (PC goals from the match page) / (PC earned from the stats PDF).
bu bahis için: Separates a team that manufactures corners from one that scores them. A specialist drag-flicker is a step change in conversion and does not show up in any team rating.
goal route split
Share of goals from field goal vs penalty corner vs penalty stroke, per team and per player.
bu bahis için: Two teams with identical goals-per-game are different bets if one scores 60% from corners: corner-dependent scoring is streakier and umpire-dependent.
cards and suspensions
Green (2 min), yellow (umpire-set duration, typically 5 or 10 min) and red cards, with minute and issuing umpire.
bu bahis için: Field hockey suspensions are long relative to a 60-minute game, and a 10-minute yellow is a sixth of the match played a player down. Card-prone teams leak corners.
shootout record
Team record in penalty shoot-outs (the 8-second one-on-one format) and stroke competitions.
bu bahis için: Decides knockout progression markets and any market Betwinner settles including shoot-outs. Goalkeeper-specific and close to independent of open-play strength.

araştırma notları
tms.fih.ch is the single best source in this file and it is fully robots-permissive. It tags EVERY field hockey goal as Penalty Corner, Field Goal or Penalty Stroke, and its per-match statistics PDF carries penalty corners PER QUARTER for both teams - which is exactly the one statistic field hockey cannot be modelled without.
But read the payload, not the template: that same PDF also contains Circle Entries, Shots, Shots on Target and Possession % and they are ZERO in every competition sampled. The fields exist and are never filled. A parser that trusts the schema would silently model four phantom statistics.
The same trap repeats in lacrosse from a different vendor: the NCAA team-stats JSON exposes faceoffsWon, faceoffsLost, clears and clearAttempts and all four read zero even in the 2025 Division I championship final. Faceoff % and clear % must be COUNTED FROM PLAY-BY-PLAY TEXT instead - the events are there ('Faceoff X vs Y won by UMD'), just not the aggregates.
len.org is the cleanest status-code trap encountered: robots.txt returns 200 with 'Allow: /' and even advertises an LLM-Policy, but the body is a redirect to a GoDaddy lander and llms.txt states the domain is for sale. A permissive robots.txt on a dead domain. len.eu separately answers 202 behind a Sucuri CAPTCHA.
Water polo is the weakest sport in this repo by data availability. Fixtures yes, scores partly, statistics no. The World Aquatics API is genuinely good at what it does - 271 water polo competitions, full schedule with phases and result status - but it returns no scores and no stats, and /results, /matches and /standings all 404.
NCAA publishes water polo SCORES ONLY: the scoreboard JSON serves fine while every boxscore, team-stats and play-by-play call 502s from the NCAA origin, verified on two separate games in a session where lacrosse calls succeeded. That is an origin gap, not our block.
Two of the three sports' best independent sources are lost to robots policy, not to technology: total-waterpolo.com and premierlacrosseleague.com both name ClaudeBot (PLL also names anthropic-ai) with Disallow: /. Wikipedia is the compliant workaround for PLL because we fetch Wikipedia, not PLL.
The draw question splits the three cleanly and decides the safety ladder: water polo and field hockey can both end drawn in pool play, so their ladders start at double chance; lacrosse resolves every tie in sudden-victory overtime, so it is a two-way market whose only de-risking rung is the handicap.
Pace in all three sports is set by the RULE SET, not the league's reputation - water polo moved to a 28-second clock in 2025 with 18-second resets, NCAA lacrosse runs 20+60, PLL runs 52 or 32 depending on how possession was won, and box runs 30. Fit totals models per regime and refresh every water polo baseline built before 2025.
PLL two-point goals break the integer-step assumption that every totals, handicap and winning-margin model in this repo currently makes. It is the only code in scope where a single scoring event moves the scoreboard by two.
Field hockey card durations are umpire-set, not fixed: green is 2 minutes at international level (sometimes only a warning nationally) and yellow is whatever the umpire decides, commonly 5 or 10. The TMS match page records the actual minutes, so the real distribution is learnable rather than assumed.

market structure note
HEAD-TO-HEAD IS THE ONLY TWO-OUTCOME MARKET IN ANY OF THESE FOUR SPORTS. Outright winner, podium, top-6/top-10 and nation markets are all many-outcome fields of 30-120 competitors. Any staking or safety-ladder logic that assumes a two-outcome, near-even-money market can therefore ONLY be applied to head-to-heads - and even there only once the non-starter void rule is confirmed, because a void leg breaks a ladder's assumed sequence. Do not port two-outcome logic onto podium or top-10 markets; they are multinomial and their prices do not complete to a simple complement.
head to head note
The only two-outcome market here, and the only one where a favourite's edge is not diluted by field size. Two effects dominate and both are structural rather than skill-based: (1) NON-STARTER RISK - if either named athlete does not start the market almost certainly voids, so the real question is P(both start), which federation news drives and no feed reports; (2) DNF/DSQ ASYMMETRY - in slalom and in ski jumping a H2H is substantially a bet on which athlete completes, and completion probability differs far more between athletes than finishing position does. Rank-based models systematically misprice H2Hs containing one high-variance athlete.
top 10 note
Field size and attrition rate change this bet completely between disciplines. In a World Cup slalom a large share of the field records no second run, so a top-10 is heavily a survival bet and the effective field is far smaller than the start list. In biathlon a top-10 is dominated by the shooting component, and the number of bouts (2 in a sprint, 4 elsewhere) sets how much shooting variance is in play. In a ski jumping competition only the top 30 after round 1 jump again, so a top-10 requires clearing that cut first - a two-stage conditional, not a single ranking draw.
nation note
Relay and team events are the natural nation market. Use the relay-INCLUSIVE podium statistics (WCPOD, TOPREL, TOPSMR) here, and the relay-EXCLUSIVE ones (WCPODI, TOPPODI) for individual markets - IBU ships both and mixing them biases toward strong relay nations. Nation markets are also where the wax-correlation effect is a genuine edge rather than noise, because service-crew quality is a nation-level trait that persists across a season. In biathlon relays, spare rounds mean a nation's shooting depth matters more than its best shooter.
araştırma notları
The IBU sport API at biathlonresults.com/modules/sportapi/api/ is the single best source in this file: fully open JSON, no key, no robots.txt on the host at all, and it ships results, per-race format metadata, season shooting and skiing statistics, cup standings and venue altitude. Endpoint list recovered from the site's own JS bundle: Results, Competitions, Events, Seasons, Cups, CupResults, Stats, StatItems, Athletes, AllResults, CISBios, AnalyticResults, MedalResults, Reports2, SearchResults, Categories, Disciplines, EventTypes, Organizers, SessionInfo, Updates.
FIS's ASP.NET-style 404s are a verification trap in both directions. Missing a REQUIRED query parameter on the IBU API returns 'No HTTP resource was found that matches the request URI' - which reads as a dead endpoint but is really a missing-parameter route failure; api/Stats needs all seven of StatisticId, StatId, byWhat, SeasonId, OrganizerId, GenderId, IBUId, Nat before it returns its 169 rows. Conversely FIS serves HTTP 404 with a 190-250 KB body of the WRONG page for URLs that do not exist. Neither status codes nor body size qualify a source here - only reading the content does.
Wikidata SPARQL returned HTTP 200, correct content-type and perfectly valid JSON with an EMPTY bindings array on my first query, purely because the class QID was wrong. This is the cleanest example in this research of why a source must be qualified on its payload: every signal short of counting the array said success.
Ski jumping gate and wind compensation points - the fields without which no ski jumping model is valid - exist in NO free structured feed. They are published only inside the per-competition Swiss Timing PDFs on fis-ski.com, together with the hill's Gate Factor, head/tail Wind Factors, meter value, K-point and HS. The FIS HTML results table carries distance, points and round rank but no gate or wind columns at all.
The distance-only model is empirically refuted, not just theoretically: at Lillehammer on 22 Nov 2025 Raimund's 139.5m was the longest jump in the group and finished 8th, while Kraft won on 138.0m, the difference coming from 4.5 judges points and a one-gate compensation gap worth 6.1 points.
Ski jumping and nordic combined have NO FIS points list whatsoever - verified, the page soft-404s. Alpine and cross-country both export a full CSV free (10,078 and 10,675 rows) with per-discipline points AND positions: DH/SL/GS/SG/AC for alpine, Distance/Sprint for cross-country. For jumping and nordic combined, cup-standings.html is the only ranking source.
Cancellation is the base case, not the tail. All five ski jumping competitions at Lake Placid in December 2025 were cancelled outright; the 2025-26 ski jumping season page records 16 cancellations and 5 reschedulings, and the alpine season page 16 and 19. A 'will be classified' assumption is unsafe in every one of these four sports, and the cancelled Ruka event was eventually run at Lahti as ONE ROUND ONLY - which settles normally while behaving statistically like a different competition.
Head-to-head is the ONLY two-outcome market in these disciplines; outright, podium, top-10 and nation markets are all 30-120-runner multinomials. Any safety-ladder or two-outcome staking logic is confined to head-to-heads - and even there a non-starter void breaks the ladder's sequence, so the void rule must be confirmed before that logic is trusted.
Biathlon's miss cost varies about threefold by format - a full added minute in the individual versus a ~20-25s penalty loop everywhere else - and the IBU exposes it machine-readably as PenaltySeconds per race. Shooting percentage therefore cannot be converted into win probability without first reading the format.
Prone versus standing shooting percentage is NOT published as a split. IBU's SHOOT_* statistic is aggregate only; the split must be derived from the per-race Shootings string, whose positional order is format-dependent (sprint P,S; pursuit and mass start P,P,S,S; individual P,S,P,S). Decoding with the wrong order silently mislabels half the observations.
Wax and ski preparation is a nation-level correlated shock rather than athlete-level noise, which both widens the whole field's distribution in marginal conditions near 0C and makes same-nation multi-athlete positions far more correlated than their individual prices imply.
Betwinner's own terms remain unread and are the largest single gap in this file. Every settlement statement here is industry convention, and in sports where shortening and one-round fallbacks are routine, the shortened-event and non-starter clauses are the difference between a live bet and a void.
